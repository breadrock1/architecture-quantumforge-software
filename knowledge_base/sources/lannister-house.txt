House Lannister of Casterly Rock is a Great House of Westeros, one of the wealthiest and most powerful families in the Seven Kingdoms. It was briefly a royal house until its defeat in the Last War. The Lannisters rule the Westerlands. Their ancestral seat is Casterly Rock, located on a massive rocky promontory overlooking the Sunset Sea. They are the Lords Paramount of the Westerlands and the Wardens of the West. As a royal house, they also directly rule the Crownlands from the Red Keep in King's Landing, the traditional seat of the royal family. The crest of House Lannister is a golden lion on a crimson field, and their motto is "Hear me roar!", which is rarely mentioned. Their unofficial motto, which is also known as the official one, "A Lannister always pays his debts," is used much more often and mostly in a negative context.

Cersei and Jaime's incestuous affair was kept secret. Their son Joffrey Baratheon laid claim to the Iron Throne, claiming that his father was actually the late King Robert Baratheon. Lord Tywin was one of the main supporters of Joffrey's rule in the War of the Five Kings. The Lannisters continued to hold power when Tommen Baratheon ascended to the throne, with Kevan Lannister becoming Hand of the King and Protector of the Realm, but lost control of Tommen due to his adherence to the Faith of the Seven, and Cersei took the throne after the destruction of the Great Sept of Baelor. Following the deaths of Cersei and Jaime at the Battle of King's Landing, Tyrion became head of House Lannister and renounced the crown in favor of an elective monarchy. He pledged allegiance directly to King Bran Stark of the Andals and First Men.
