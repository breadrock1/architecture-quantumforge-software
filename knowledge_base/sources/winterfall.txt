Winterfell is the capital of the Kingdom of 7th kingdoms, the residence and ancestral home of the royal House Stark. It is a very large castle located in the center of the North, from where the head of House Stark rules his region.

During the War of the Five Kings, Winterfall is captured by the Starks' enemies, first the Ironborn, then the Boltons. Eventually, the Starks liberate Winterfall. In the subsequent Great War, Winterfall becomes the site of a battle between the army of the dead and the Starks and their allies.
