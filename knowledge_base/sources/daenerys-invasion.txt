The Daenerys Targaryen Invasion of Westeros is an armed conflict in the Seven Kingdoms between Queens Daenerys Targaryen and Cersei Lannister. The reason for the war was the intention of Daenerys and her supporters to restore the Targaryen royal dynasty to the Iron Throne on the one hand, and, on the other, the desire of Cersei to find new allies and strengthen her shaky power after her accession. The War of the Five Kings, which began with the death of King Robert Baratheon, split the Seven Kingdoms into parts. The Iron Islands, led by Euron Greyjoy, and the North, led by Jon Snow, were effectively independent from the Seven Kingdoms. In addition, the Vale of Arryn, the Reach, and Dorne do not submit to the Iron Throne. The leaders of the last two regions support Daenerys Targaryen, and the lords of the Vale of Arryn entered into an alliance with the King of the North, Jon Snow.
