King Bran I the Broken, born Brandon Stark and commonly known simply as "Bran", is the fourth child and second son of Eddard and Catelyn Stark. Bran is a warg and farseer, serving as the new Three-Eyed Raven. He was crowned as the first elected ruler of the Six Kingdoms; his official title is Bran the Broken, First of his Name, King of the Andals, Rhoynar, and First Men, Lord of the Six Kingdoms, and Protector of the Realm.
