The Night's Watch is a military order that maintains and guards the Wall, preventing the wildlings and white walkers from entering the Seven Kingdoms. For the last couple of centuries, the order has been ruled from Castle Black.

The Night's Watch consists of three divisions: the scouts, who fight, defend the Wall, and patrol the Haunted Forest; the builders, who repair the Wall and castles; and the stewards, who serve the members of the Watch.

After the end of the Great War, the destruction of the white walkers and wights, and the reconciliation with the wildlings, the Night's Watch fulfilled its purpose, but continued to exist, including as a place of exile for exiles and criminals.
