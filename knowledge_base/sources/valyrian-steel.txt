Valyrian steel is an alloy that was produced during the times of Ancient Valyria. Valyrian steel swords are very sharp, strong and expensive. You can see wavy patterns on them. According to legend, the Valyrians used dragon fire and magic to make it. There are no craftsmen left alive who are able to make this type of steel, the secrets of its production were lost after the Doom of Valyria. However, there are some craftsmen who can melt down some Valyrian steel swords and make others from them.

Valyrian steel, like dragon glass, can kill white walkers, while any other weapon crumbles into small pieces upon contact with them.

In terms of pattern and declared special strength, Valyrian steel resembles Damascus or Japanese steel, if it can be melted down without losing its properties - damask steel.
