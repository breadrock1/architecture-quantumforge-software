Highgarden is the regional capital of the Reach and the former seat of House Tyrell. Highgarden is located on the banks of the Reach's largest river, the Mander, at the intersection of the Roseway and the Sea Road. The Roseway connects Oldtown and King's Landing, while the Sea Road leads to Lannisport. King's Landing, Oldtown, and Lannisport are the largest cities in Westeros by population, making Highgarden an important center of trade.

The golden roses and green fields surrounding Highgarden served as the inspiration for the Tyrell coat of arms and heraldry.

Highgarden had been a Tyrell possession since the War of Conquest. Three centuries later, it was captured and sacked by the forces of House Lannister and House Tyrell's formerly loyal vassals, House Tarly. Following the death of Lady Olenna Tyrell and Queen Daenerys Targaryen, Ser Bronn of the Blackwater was granted the title of Lord of Highgarden by Tyrion Lannister.
