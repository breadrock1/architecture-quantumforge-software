The Faith of the Seven is the primary religion of the Seven Kingdoms. It has little presence outside of the kingdom. The Faith of the Seven is dominant in the south of Westeros. The only regions where it is not the majority religion are the North, where the Old Gods are still worshiped, and the Iron Islands, where the Drowned God is the most common religion. The Faith of the Seven, its influence, and its followers suffered greatly due to the destruction of its religious center, the Great Sept of Baelor, by wildfire as part of a plot orchestrated by Cersei Lannister.
