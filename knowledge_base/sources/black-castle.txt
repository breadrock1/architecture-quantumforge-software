Castle Black is the headquarters and redoubt of the Night's Watch. It is located roughly halfway along the Wall on its southern side. The kingsroad ends near the castle. Castle Black is one of three inhabited castles on the Wall, the others being Eastwatch by the Sea and the Shadow Tower. There are nineteen castles on the Wall, but over the centuries the Night's Watch has abandoned sixteen of them. Castle Black is located between the abandoned castles of Queen's Gate to the west and Oakenshield to the east. Castle Black has a sept, but no godswood. New recruits to the Night's Watch, who worship the Old Gods, must travel a mile north of the Wall into the Haunted Forest to swear their vows before the weirwood.

Upon learning of the Wall's destruction, Jon Snow orders the remaining castles on the Wall abandoned and their garrisons brought to Winterfall.
