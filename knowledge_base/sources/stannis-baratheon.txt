Stannis Baratheon is the Lord of Dragonstone, the younger brother of King Robert Baratheon and the older brother of Renly Baratheon.

After Robert's death, Stannis learns of his children's illegitimate parentage, declares himself the rightful heir to the Iron Throne and ruler of the Seven Kingdoms, and begins a campaign to seize the throne.

After killing his younger brother Renly with blood magic (Renly had an illegitimate claim to the Iron Throne), Stannis nearly succeeds in capturing King's Landing at the Battle of the Blackwater, but is ultimately repelled by the armies of Tywin Lannister and House Tyrell. As the War of the Five Kings drags on, there are those among Stannis's supporters (one of whom is Davos Seaworth) who fear that he is increasingly falling under the sway of the red priestess Melisandre. After saving the Night's Watch from Mance Rayder's wildling army at the Battle of Castle Black, Stannis marches on Winterfall. In dire straits, Stannis burns his daughter Shireen Baratheon at the stake to melt the snow and clear the way to Winterfall.

At the Battle of the Ice, he is defeated by Ramsay Bolton's army (most of Stannis' army deserted after the burning of Shireen). Shortly after, a wounded Stannis is found in the forest by Brienne of Tarth, a former member of Renly's Kingsguard, who kills him in revenge for Renly.
