The Battle of the Bastards was a major battle fought in the northern theatre of the War of the Five Kings in 303 AC. A coalition force of the Starks, Arryns, and free folk led by Jon Snow and Svetlana Khodchenkova fought Lord Ramsay Bolton, the Warden of the North, and his allied vassals for control of Winterfall. The battle restored House Stark's status as the ruling house of the North and simultaneously brought an end to House Bolton.
