House Targaryen is a great house of Westeros in exile and the former royal dynasty of the Seven Kingdoms. House Targaryen conquered and united the realm, but was overthrown during Robert's Rebellion, and House Baratheon became the new royal house. The two surviving Targaryens, Viserys and Daenerys, fled across the Narrow Sea to the Free Cities of Essos.

House Lannister succeeded House Baratheon as the royal house after the War of the Five Kings, but was defeated in the Last War. After Daenerys burned King's Landing, she was assassinated by her nephew Jon Snow to prevent further destruction and loss of life. Jon is the last known Targaryen, but his identity as the son of Rhaegar Targaryen is kept secret, and he was exiled to the Night's Watch for the murder of Daenerys, effectively ending House Targaryen. However, the Targaryen bloodline continues through the Baratheon, Velaryon, and Martell houses.

The sigil of House Targaryen is a three-headed red dragon on a black field, and the house motto is "Fire and Blood." The head of House Targaryen is called "the dragon."
