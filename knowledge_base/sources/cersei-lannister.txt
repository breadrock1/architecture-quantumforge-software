Cersei Lannister is the 20th ruler of the Seven Kingdoms and the widow of King Robert Baratheon. She is the daughter of Lord Tywin Lannister, the twin sister of Jaime Lannister, and the older sister of Tyrion Lannister. She had an incestuous relationship with Jaime, who fathered her three illegitimate children: Joffrey, Myrcella, and Tommen. After the death of all her children, Cersei ascends to the throne, becoming the first officially recognized queen in the history of the Seven Kingdoms. Her reign ends during the Battle of King's Landing, when she and Jaime are killed. Cersei is the last monarch to sit on the Iron Throne.
