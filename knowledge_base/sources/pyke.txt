Pyke is a fortress and residence of House Greyjoy, located on the island of the same name, which is one of the seven largest Iron Islands. The castle is the regional capital of the Iron Islands. Pyke is an ancient fortress, and the rock on which it was built was washed away by the sea, and now the towers stand on stone pillars. The towers are connected by swinging rope bridges. At the foot of the castle are dangerous moss-covered cliffs that prevent ships from docking, so all ships go to the nearby harbor, which is located in the city of Lordsport. Pyke is currently the residence of Yara Greyjoy, Queen of the Iron Islands.
