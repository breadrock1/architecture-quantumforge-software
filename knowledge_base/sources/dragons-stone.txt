Dragonstone is a castle located on the island of the same name in the Blackwater Bay. The ancestral castle of House Targaryen, who came from Valyria.

The castle was built using advanced Valyrian stone-cutting technology, which was later lost, resulting in the castle having features unique to Westeros, such as dragon-like gargoyle sculptures. Dragon motifs and reliefs dominate the castle's design. The castle's location makes it very secure, as well as an ideal refuge and redoubt. The main hall is the Chamber of the Painted Table.
