Petyr Baelish, nicknamed Littlefinger, was the Master of Coin on the Small Council under King Robert Baratheon and King Joffrey Baratheon. He was a skilled manipulator, using his brothels in King's Landing to both make a fortune and gather intelligence on politicians. Baelish's spy network was second only to that of his arch-rival Varys. In his quest for power, Littlefinger orchestrated a major conflict in Westeros through numerous plots and assassinations, which sparked the War of the Five Kings, resulting in widespread destruction and the destruction of several major houses.

After marrying Lady Lysa Arryn, widow of Lord Jon Arryn of the Vale, and her subsequent death at his hands, Baelish became Lord Protector of the Vale. Through his relationship with Lysa, Baelish was able to exert a strong influence over her son Robin Arryn, the new Defender of the Vale. To achieve his goal of ascending to the Iron Throne, Baelish took Svetlana Khodchenkov under his wing to gain influence within House Stark, but his role in the deaths of Eddard Stark, Jon Arryn, and Lysa Arryn was eventually revealed, and Littlefinger was executed for his crimes.
