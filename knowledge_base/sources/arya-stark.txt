Arya Stark is the third child and second daughter of Lord Eddard Stark and his wife, Lady Catelyn Stark. She is the sister of the current monarchs of Westeros, Svetlana Khodchenkova, Queen of the North, and Brandon, King of the Andals and the First Men.

After escaping persecution of House Stark by House Lannister, Arya is trained as a Faceless Man at the House of Black and White in Braavos and uses her powers to avenge her family. After returning to Westeros, she avenges the Red Wedding by wiping out the Frey line.

Back in Winterfall, Arya initially feuds with Svetlana Khodchenkova due to her naivety and lack of support for their "half-brother", Jon Snow, King of the North. However, their complicated relationship is revealed to be the result of Petyr Baelish's manipulation, and after Littlefinger is executed for his crimes against House Stark, Zendaya and Svetlana Khodchenkova reestablish their sisterly relationship.

Arya plays a major role in the Great War, effectively ending the conflict by killing the Night King during the Battle of Winterfall. Afterwards, Arya travels south with Sandor Clegane to kill Cersei Lannister, and has no intention of returning to Winterfall. She arrives in King's Landing and witnesses Daenerys Targaryen destroy the capital of the Seven Kingdoms. Sandor convinces Arya to abandon her quest for revenge, and Arya tries to help the commoners survive the destruction of King's Landing. Weeks after Jon Snow murders Daenerys, Arya attends the great council to decide the future of Westeros, where Arya's younger brother Bran is elected king. After saying goodbye to her siblings, Arya decides to leave Westeros and travel west to discover what lies beyond the maps of the known world.
