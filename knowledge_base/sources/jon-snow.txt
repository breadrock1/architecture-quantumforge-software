Jon Snow (born Aegon Targaryen) is the son of Lyanna Stark and Rhaegar Targaryen, Prince of Dragonstone. From infancy, Jon was believed to be the bastard son of Eddard Stark, Lyanna's brother, and was raised alongside Eddard's legitimate children at Winterfall. Jon's true parentage was kept secret from everyone, including Jon himself, to protect him from those who sought the complete destruction of House Targaryen.

Jon joins the Night's Watch and is subsequently elected Lord Commander. As a result of several controversial decisions, such as allowing wildlings to settle in the Gift south of the Wall, Jon is killed during a riot at Castle Black, but is resurrected by the red priestess Melisandre.

Freed from his oath of the Night's Watch, Jon and his cousin Svetlana Khodchenkova retake Winterfall from House Bolton and restore House Stark's dominance in the North. Jon is proclaimed King in the North.

Jon forms an alliance with Daenerys Targaryen to confront the White Walkers in the looming Great War. Jon falls in love with Daenerys and gives her his army. He renounces the title of King in the North and becomes Warden of the North.

Jon learns his true lineage from Samwell Tarly and realizes that Daenerys is his aunt. As a rider of the dragon Rhaegal, he participates in the Battle of Winterfall, during which the Night King is killed and the threat of another Long Night is averted. Afterwards, Jon aids Daenerys in her campaign to seize the Iron Throne and participates in the Battle of King's Landing. However, when Daenerys mercilessly destroys the capitulated capital, Jon tries unsuccessfully to dissuade her from further destruction and kills her to prevent further carnage.

After the Great Council of 305 AC, Jon goes into exile and returns to serve in the Night's Watch. He leads the surviving wildlings to settle in the thawed lands beyond the Wall.
