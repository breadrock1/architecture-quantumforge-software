House Baratheon is a Great House of Westeros that rules the Stormlands on the eastern coast of Westeros, named for its frequent storms. The Baratheon family seat is Storm's End.

House Baratheon became the royal house of the Seven Kingdoms after Robert Baratheon led a rebellion against the Targaryen dynasty. At the end of the rebellion, Robert ascended to the Iron Throne as Robert I and married Cersei Lannister after the death of Lyanna Stark.

House Baratheon officially died out after the death of Stannis Baratheon and his family, but was revived when Robert's last known bastard was legitimized by Queen Daenerys Targaryen as Gendry Baratheon.

The crest of House Baratheon is a black stag with a crown on a field of gold, and the motto is "We are the Fury".
