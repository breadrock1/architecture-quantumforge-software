Ser Brienne of Tarth is a knight of House Tarth and the only daughter of Lord Selwyn of Tarth.

Before the Battle of Winterfall, Brienne was knighted by Ser Jaime Lannister, making her the first woman in the Seven Kingdoms to become a knight. Following the election of Bran I the Broken as King of the Andals and the death of Daenerys Targaryen, Brienne is appointed the new Lord Commander of the Kingsguard.
