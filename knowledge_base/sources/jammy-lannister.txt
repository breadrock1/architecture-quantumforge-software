Jaime Lannister is the eldest son of Lord Tywin Lannister, the younger twin brother of Queen Cersei Lannister, and the older brother of Tyrion Lannister. Since childhood, Jaime had an incestuous relationship with Cersei, who bore him three illegitimate children: Joffrey, Myrcella, and Tommen.

During Robert's Rebellion, Jaime killed Aerys Targaryen, for which he received the derogatory nickname "Kingslayer". Robert pardoned him and allowed him to continue serving in the Kingsguard. After King Robert's death, he became Lord Commander of the Kingsguard to his son Joffrey.

During the War of the Five Kings, Jaime fought for his house, but was captured by Robb Stark and taken prisoner. He came to an agreement with Catelyn Stark, who agreed to release him on the condition that her daughters were safely returned to her. Catelyn tasked Brienne of Tarth with bringing Jaime to the capital. During the journey, Jaime lost his right hand and revealed the true reasons for his murder of Aerys. After returning to the capital, he continued to serve as Lord Commander under Joffrey and then Tommen, but was removed from office after a tense standoff with the Faith Militant. He became the new commander of the Lannister forces, but left his post to help the Northerners confront the army of the dead. After defeating the dead, he headed to the capital to help his sister. Jaime died during the Battle of King's Landing while trying to smuggle Cersei out of the capital.
