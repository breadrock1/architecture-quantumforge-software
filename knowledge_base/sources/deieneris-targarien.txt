Daenerys Targaryen, also known as Daenerys Stormborn, is the only daughter of King Aerys II Targaryen and Queen Rhaella, and the younger sister of Rhaegar and Viserys Targaryen.

In her youth, Daenerys was a timid and obedient girl. After marrying Drogo, she gained the trust of his khalasar. Following her husband's death, Daenerys expanded her influence across Essos, winning the loyalty of the Unsullied army and liberating Slaver's Bay from Ghiscari slavers.

During the Last War, Daenerys suffered personal loss, grief, and betrayal before eventually succumbing to the so-called Targaryen madness that had plagued her ancestors. After sacked King's Landing despite its surrender, Daenerys seized power by killing Queen Cersei Lannister and many commoners. Daenerys' nephew Jon Snow failed to dissuade her from further destruction and killed her to prevent further slaughter. The new king was Bran the Broken, chosen at the Great Council of 305.
