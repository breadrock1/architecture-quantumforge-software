The Iron Throne is the name of the throne on which the King of the Andals, Rhoynar, and First Men sits. It is located in the Great Hall of the Red Keep, located in King's Landing. The throne may be occupied by the King himself, his regent, or, if they are absent from the capital, the Hand of the King. The term "Iron Throne" is also often used to refer to the monarchy that rules the Seven Kingdoms, as well as the power of the King.
